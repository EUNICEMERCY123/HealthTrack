Help & Limits

How to use

  1. Log your vitals on the Home page whenever you measure them.
  2. Use Symptoms to see what your symptoms may point to.
  3. Use Stroke Risk for a simple low, medium or high signal.
  4. Open My Records to see trends and show the list to a doctor.

Please remember

  • This is a screening and tracking tool. It is not a diagnosis. Only a doctor can tell you what is wrong.
  • Emergency? Sudden chest pain, trouble breathing, face drooping, weak arm or slurred speech: call 112 or go to the nearest hospital now.
  • The symptom model learned from a practice dataset where each disease has very clean, repeated symptom lists. Real people are messier, so treat the answer as a hint.
  • The stroke model is tuned to catch most strokes, so it gives many false alarms. The score is a signal, not a true chance.
  • Your records stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing browser data erases them.
