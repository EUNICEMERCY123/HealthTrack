Learn

Simple answers to common questions.

What is diabetes?

Diabetes means the body has trouble keeping blood sugar (glucose) at a healthy level. Many people have no signs at first, so early screening helps.

Why does blood sugar matter most?

In the data this app learned from, blood sugar was the strongest clue. People with diabetes had clearly higher blood sugar on average.

What is BMI?

BMI is your weight (kg) divided by your height (m) squared. It is a quick way to see if weight may be adding to your health risk.

Healthy habits that help
  • Move your body most days, for example a brisk walk.
  • Choose home-cooked meals with vegetables and whole grains.
  • Cut down on sugary drinks and sweets.
  • Sleep well and manage stress.
  • Get regular check-ups.
When should I see a doctor?

If your result is Medium or High, if you have family history, or if you often feel very thirsty, tired or pass urine more than usual, please see a doctor for a proper blood test.

How does this app work?

It uses Logistic Regression, a method that gives each fact a weight, adds them up and turns the total into a chance between 0% and 100%. It was trained on 768 records from a public dataset.

Which blood sugar number do I use?

Use a recent glucose result in mg/dL from a lab or clinic. The data behind this app used a 2-hour glucose test, so readings from other tests are only approximate.

How to read your result
  • LOW: few risk signs in the numbers you gave.
  • MEDIUM: some risk signs. A doctor's test is a good idea.
  • HIGH: many risk signs. Please see a doctor soon.
Important limits
  • This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.
  • It was built from records of women aged 21 and above, so results for other people may be less reliable.
  • "Not sure" answers use typical values and make the result less accurate.
  • Family history is an approximate answer, not an exact score.
Your privacy

Everything is worked out on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. Saved check-ins stay on this device and you can delete them any time.
